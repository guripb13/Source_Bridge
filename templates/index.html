💡Quick tips
Hold Alt and drag any message or reply to drop it anywhere: another app, a text editor, or the input box.
Press Enter to send, Shift + Enter for a new line.
Pasting long code? Click the expand icon in the top-right of the input box to make it taller.
Pick the target language with the button next to the paperclip. Choose Other to type your own.
Click the copy icon next to your message, or under a reply, to copy it. On older messages, hover to reveal it.
